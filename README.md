## About me

Hello! I'm **Gustavo Moura** — I share my projects, ideas and learnings here. I'm currently studying **Analysis & Systems Development** at Senac, a program connected to the **Porto Digital** ecosystem. I also study English at Senac to strengthen my professional communication.

I have a strong passion for web development with a focus on Back‑End, especially using **Java & Spring Boot**, but I also enjoy exploring Front‑End technologies to deliver complete solutions and a great user experience. I work with APIs, system modeling and integrations, and I'm always learning new tools and best practices.
